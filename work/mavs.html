Project 19

Dallas Mavericks Yearbook

A season-defining yearbook for the 2020–21 Dallas Mavericks — an editorial system built to hold an entire season between two covers. Player features for Luka Dončić and Kristaps Porzingis sit alongside a full walk-through of the uniform program, from the Earned and City Editions to the return of the Hardwood Classic Kelly Green. Bold display type, the team's blue-and-gold palette, and cinematic photography turn a record of the season into a keepsake.
